Asset paths below refer to the source archive.

DSi Shop icon and cover

assets/dsi-shop-icon.png and common/shop_icon.h contain the 32x32
Nintendo DSi Shop banner icon.
assets/icon.bmp is the corresponding 16-color application banner bitmap.
assets/pico/DSiStudio.nds.bmp is Studio's Pico cover and includes Nintendo
branding and a representation of the console and Studio interface.
Nintendo artwork and branding are not covered by the project's GPL code license.
DSi Studio is an independent project and is not endorsed by Nintendo.

Interface font

common/font.h contains ASCII glyphs converted from the included public-domain
X11 misc-fixed 6x10 font. See licenses/x11-fixed.txt for its source, original
notice and conversion details.

Audio

The music and UI effects in assets/audio are original procedural compositions
generated by tools/make_audio.py. The current music is the original Shop
composition introduced in Studio 0.4.4. The script and generated audio are provided
under the project's GPL-3.0-or-later license. No Nintendo audio is included.
