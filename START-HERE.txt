DSi Studio 0.4.2
===============

Read this before copying files to your card.

You need a DSi and charger, a working DSpico with DSi storage access,
and your own complete DSiWare game folders. Studio runs offline.

Installing changes the DSi's internal storage. A failed write can leave it
unable to start. Studio requires a backup but cannot restore it. A backup
alone does not provide recovery. This is experimental software.

1. EXTRACT THE DOWNLOAD
   Windows: right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
   Mac: double-click the ZIP.

2. COPY STUDIO TO YOUR CARD
   Power off your DSi. Put the DSpico's microSD in a computer card reader.
   Open COPY TO SD CARD in the extracted download.
   Copy the Games, DSiWare and _pico folders inside it to the first folder
   of your microSD. Merge their contents; keep existing files.
   Do not replace or delete the card's entire _pico folder.

   If your computer offers to replace a whole folder, cancel and copy
   the files individually:
     Games/DSiStudio.nds
     _pico/covers/user/DSiStudio.nds.bmp
   Create the folders if they do not exist. Also create DSiWare.

   Updating? Save the old DSiStudio.nds on your computer before replacing
   that file. Leave your DSiStudio/backups folder on the card intact.

3. ADD YOUR GAME FOLDERS
   Drag your complete game folders into DSiWare on the card.
   Each unpacked game needs a matching .tmd file beside it, for example:

     DSiWare/My Game/Game.nds
     DSiWare/My Game/Game.tmd

   Names before the dot must match. The .tmd must belong to that exact
   game and version. Studio does not download missing files or open ZIPs.

4. OPEN STUDIO
   Eject the microSD using your computer's eject option. Return it to the
   DSpico and insert the DSpico into your powered-off DSi.
   Connect the charger, turn on the DSi, then open Games > DSi Studio.
   Choose Open library. If it says Browsing only, see the help link below.

5. BACK UP THIS DSi
   Follow the backup prompt. Saving and checking can take several minutes.
   Keep the charger and card connected until both steps finish.
   Before installing games, power off and copy the card's entire
   DSiStudio/backups folder to your computer. Keep the original on the card.
   Each DSi has its own backup folder; keep them all when using several DSis.

6. INSTALL
   Return the card to the DSpico and reopen Studio. It checks the backup
   once per session. Choose a game and select Copy to DSi.
   Read the message, then hold A or Hold to copy until copying starts.
   Keep power and the card connected. Wait for Added to your DSi.
   Choose Done to install another game without restarting.

7. PLAY
   When finished, power off, remove the DSpico and turn the DSi on normally.
   Open the installed game from the Home Menu.

Full guide: https://github.com/ShinMikite/DSi-Studio/blob/main/docs/GETTING-STARTED.md
Help: https://github.com/ShinMikite/DSi-Studio/blob/main/docs/HELP.md

If a copy fails, note the exact message and ask for help before retrying.
Do not upload games, console backups, console identity files or private keys.
