NAND Title Manager (NTM)
https://github.com/Epicpkmn11/NTM
Cryptographic sources and headers are preserved under vendor/ntm-crypto.
ORIGIN.txt records the exact upstream revision. The save formatter derives
from NTM with additional error checks. Original authorship and license
headers, including earlier cryptographic implementations, are retained.
The inherited save-formatting algorithm also credits d0k3/GodMode9 in sav.c.

Backup format
https://github.com/zoogie/dumpTool
Studio's backup writer follows the no$gba footer layout and console-identity
verification approach documented by dumpTool and the DSi homebrew community.
