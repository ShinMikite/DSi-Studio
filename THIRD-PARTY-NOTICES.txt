NAND Title Manager (NTM)
https://github.com/Epicpkmn11/NTM
Cryptographic sources and headers are preserved under vendor/ntm-crypto.
ORIGIN.txt records the exact upstream revision. The save formatter derives
from NTM with additional error checks. Original authorship and license
headers, including earlier cryptographic implementations, are retained.

BlocksDS / libnds
https://blocksds.skylyrac.net/
The ARM7 core retains its Zlib license header. Build files derived from
BlocksDS/NTM retain their CC0 notices. libnds and dswifi are external build
dependencies, not bundled source in this repository.

mbedTLS
https://github.com/Mbed-TLS/mbedtls
External native cryptography dependency; see the installed version's license.
